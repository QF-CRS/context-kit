# Code of Conduct

We want Context Kit to be a welcoming project for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code and ideas.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and evidence.
- Keep reports and discussions focused on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately exposing private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub.
